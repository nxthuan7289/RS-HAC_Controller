Abstract

This paper presents a Recursive Semantic Hedge Algebra Controller (RS-HAC) for nonlinear system control. The proposed controller introduces two components that are not part of classical hedge-algebra controllers: the Semantically Quantifying Simplified Mapping (SQSM), which generates ordered semantic tables for any odd label count without expert assessment of hedge effects, and the Infinite-domain General Semantization/De-semantization (IGS/IGDS), which uses the standard logistic map for states without known bounds.

We show that the parameters of each SQSM line determine its local gain in closed form, so that local exponential stability can be assigned by a linear design. The RS-HAC is validated on a cart–pole system through both simulation and real-time experiments implemented on a Teensy 4.1 microcontroller.

The proposed approach is compared with a Takagi–Sugeno Fuzzy Controller (FC) and a Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR). In simulation, RS-HAC stabilized the pendulum from 10° in 2.05 s, against 5.24 s for the FC and 2.48 s for the LQR, although the LQR used less control effort; in a 0.2 m cart translation its overshoot was 1%, against 52% and 2%.

On a physical cart–pole, the controller was tested in swing-up and stabilization, recovery from manual disturbances and cart position steps, and its control routine took 1.89 µs on average per 1 ms cycle.
